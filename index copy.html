Why Us

Why Choose
Idaho Deck?

As a locally owned and operated business, we understand the unique needs of our community. Our commitment to quality, craftsmanship, and customer satisfaction is a reflection of our dedication to serving our neighbors and friends in Idaho. When you choose us, you're choosing a partner who truly cares about your outdoor space.

Every deck is built by skilled craftsmen who care about the details, from the first measurement to the final finish.

We use only high-quality timber and composite materials, and we inspect every project to make sure it lasts.
